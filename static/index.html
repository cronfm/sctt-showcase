PYTORCH3D INCLUDED

Real structures.
Inspectable views.

The optional backend builds real Pointclouds, reads their packed RGB tensors, and transforms camera quaternions with PyTorch3D. Plotly displays the result.

The standalone example also supports plot_scene and PerspectiveCameras with a full PyTorch3D installation. Displayed orientations are provided, not predicted.
